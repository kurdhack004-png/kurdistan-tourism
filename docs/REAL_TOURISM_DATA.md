Descriptions in the seed are concise application-ready summaries, translated into Central Kurdish, Arabic and English. They are not copied verbatim from the source pages.

## Data policy

The seed intentionally does **not** invent hotels, accommodation owners, room availability, prices, ratings, payment accounts, or reviews. Those records should be entered from verified accommodation operators or an administrator.

The seeder marks the listed public tourism locations as verified because they are curated reference locations. Operational verification and field updates should still be maintained by the application's admin workflow.
